About

Edric (Ed) Tam

About

Edric (Ed) Tam is a statistician and AI researcher currently working as postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University (Advisor: Barbara Engelhardt). He received his PhD in Statistical Science from Duke University (Advisor: David Dunson). His research lies at the intersection of AI, Bayesian Statistics and Biomedical Applications. His postdoctoral research has been supported by a Croucher Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship as well as a Warren Alpert Fellowship in AI and Computational Biology.

Research Interests

Bayesian inference, computation and decision-making aided by neural networks and synthetic data, motivated by biomedicine.

  • Bayes: Robust Bayes, Generalized Bayes, Synthetic and Simulated Data (e.g. from AI systems and scientific simulators), Bayesian Computation, (Markov Chain) Monte Carlo
  • AI: Embeddings, Deep Generative Models, AI Evaluation
  • Biomedical Applications: Medical Imaging, Pharmacology, Neuroscience, Genomics etc
